Director(s)

A private company needs at least one director. BizPortal will ask for each director's ID number and a certified ID copy — have them ready.

Makhie does not ask for or store ID numbers — those go straight into BizPortal only. Your pack lists what to have on hand.

Then register — officially, quickly
